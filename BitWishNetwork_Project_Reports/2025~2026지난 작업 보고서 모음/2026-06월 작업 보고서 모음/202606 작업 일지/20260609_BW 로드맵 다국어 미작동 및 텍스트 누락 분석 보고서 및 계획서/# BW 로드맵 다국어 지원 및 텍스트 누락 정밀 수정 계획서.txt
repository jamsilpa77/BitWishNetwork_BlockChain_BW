## Proposed Changes

### 1. 관리자 도구 수정

#### [MODIFY] [AdminPage.tsx](file:///c:/BitWishNetwork_BlockChainMainnet/BitWishNetwork_MiningSystem/src/components/AdminPage/AdminPage.tsx)
